# Limitations

*Status: placeholder — expanded throughout the project.*

Standing constraints:

- Public data only; no Tesla internal systems, supplier contracts, SKU-level inventory, or confidential factory data.
- Production–delivery gap may reflect transit, timing, logistics, demand, shutdowns, product mix, or reporting cutoffs — not asserted as unsold inventory.
- Correlation is not causation; insights use qualified language.
- Missing values remain null and are flagged; no silent interpolation.
