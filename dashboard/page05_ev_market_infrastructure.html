Scope limits. CEC “new ZEV sales” are inferred from DMV registrations with methodology changes in 2023 and 2025 — treat long YoY spans cautiously. Prefer the export Data as of date over contemporaneous press totals (2025Q2 press cited 100,671; this June 30, 2026 export revises that quarter to 101,623). Charger figures are ports, not stations. Page KPIs use public ports only (Level 1 + Level 2 + DC fast). CEC’s workbook Total also includes shared-private ports (workplaces, multi-family, fleets) and excludes residential — that broader total is retained in the fact table as All_Sectors_Ports_Total but is not the headline infrastructure KPI.
