Analytical notes. Inventory is a consolidated balance-sheet total — not finished-vehicle units and not the production–delivery gap from Page 2. Inventory days use automotive COGS as the flow proxy and are labeled estimated. Q4 P&L and cash-flow fields are calculated as fiscal-year 10-K totals minus Q3 nine-month YTD; Q2/Q3 cash flows are often YTD minus prior YTD. CapEx is stored as a positive cash-outflow magnitude so Free Cash Flow = Operating Cash Flow − CapEx.
